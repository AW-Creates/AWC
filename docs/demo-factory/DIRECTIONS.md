# Choosing a real-estate direction

Choose by business fit, never rotate randomly. Each uses the same configuration, generator, prepared chat, routing, privacy and quota engine. No conversion lift has been measured.

| Direction | Use when | Composition and typography | Image, motion, trust/CTA and Crew |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial Luxury | A boutique/luxury brokerage has strong architecture and lifestyle photography | Cinematic full-width residence hero, oversized serif/italic type, asymmetric journal, editorial service rows | Immersive photography, quiet vertical entrance, explicit design-study captions, buyer/seller intent links, restrained square evergreen Crew |
| Modern Architectural | A contemporary brokerage sells a design-oriented brand and concise service structure | Split geometric hero, sans type, clipped image, numbered grid services and contrasting local-focus band | Cropped architectural studies, lateral entrance, numbered clarity rather than invented stats, direct lime CTA, sharp rectangular Crew |
| High-Producer Personal Brand | An individual/team lead can support an authentic personal approach, local story and reviewed credentials | Warm serif typography, arched lifestyle image, team-lead perspective, narrative sections and signed brand treatment | Lifestyle context, gentle scale/vertical entrance, approved biography instead of invented production rank, conversational CTA, rounded terracotta Crew |

The personal-brand direction can accommodate verified authority/production evidence later; the fictional Maren proof intentionally claims no rank, sales volume, license or testimonial. The shared channels always retain Talk Here → Call Me → Chat hierarchy and truthful availability. Reduced-motion removes entrance and hover animation. Business names, approved facts, specialist identity/role and human-review targets are config-driven.
