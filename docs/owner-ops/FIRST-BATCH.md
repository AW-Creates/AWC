# First manual multi-vertical batch — design only

No real prospect list, scraping or outreach produced in this milestone. Fictional fixtures validate the workflow across cleaning, real estate and professional services.

Start with **six manually chosen businesses: two per niche**. This is a learning batch, not a decision that cleaning is the exclusive market or that high-selling agents necessarily have budget. Expand toward 10–20 only after the first concepts reveal repeatable needs and delivery effort.

## Research fields

Record business identity, public site URL, niche, source/capture date, source-supported services/area/FAQ, observed experience or follow-up gap, current tools where publicly stated, one specific concept hypothesis, available public contact path and exact next action. Unknowns remain unknown; don't infer earnings, staffing, insurance, listing availability, fees or willingness to buy. Public facts are captured manually and reviewed; no private portal, login or mass collection.

## Transparent scoring

Each owner-entered signal is 0–5; unknown/not evidenced starts at 0. Score = sum of `signal / 5 × weight`:

| Signal | Weight | Evidence to record |
|---|---:|---|
| Website/customer experience gap | 20 | A specific visible source-supported friction point |
| Response/follow-up gap | 25 | Observable public journey gap; don't invent missed calls or test deceptive inquiries |
| Workflow fit | 25 | Clear bounded use for forms, service guidance, qualification or a reviewed request draft |
| Public contact path | 10 | A usable public business contact route; existence alone does not authorize sending |
| Evidence quality | 20 | Current traceable source information sufficient for a faithful concept |

Before review the score is provisional. After review it remains a research priority measure, not a revenue forecast or budget assessment. Record why each signal received its score in owner notes/source excerpt. Compare scores within and across niches; ties are expected. Do not rank based on unverified personal wealth.

## Batch checkpoints

Capture → verify facts/content rights → choose original template → configure a role with limited permissions → generate → QA facts/claims/layout/links/handoff boundaries → authenticated private deployment/access test → owner review. Outreach remains a separate authorization; a demo-ready workspace does not send messages.

For the first six, track preparation time, source completeness, template fit, unanswered facts, QA failures and realistic scope of a focused offer. Use those results to decide whether template work, specialist routing, CRM adapters or secure delivery deserves the next platform investment.
