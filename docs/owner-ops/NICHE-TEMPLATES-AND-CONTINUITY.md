# Industry templates and Crew conversation continuity

Owner-approved direction, 2026-10-08. Milestone: **AWC — Industry Templates & Crew Conversation Continuity**. Baseline d1c08fc41198b8dae39ece8a90451a5660c35529, clean local/origin/fresh remote main.

## Locked design direction

The owner workbench uses AWC branding. Prospect sites use their own business identity and industry conventions, never a reskin of the AWC company site. Build an original reusable template family for each niche, with separate structure, imagery/art direction, navigation, customer journeys and calls to action. Premium means clear hierarchy, relevant visual storytelling, readable content and useful next steps. Realtor sites should feel like realtor sites; cleaning sites should feel like cleaning businesses. More variants can follow evidence from prospects; this slice delivers one distinct family per supported niche.

Business facts, specialist configuration and layout remain separate. HTML/CSS and lightweight JavaScript are sufficient for this local concept milestone. No new paid dependency or template purchase. No imported proprietary template code or assets. Original illustrative artwork may stand in for licensed photography during synthetic QA; real prospect concepts need appropriate business-specific imagery and verified rights. Never fabricate listings, testimonials, credentials, availability or client results.

These are structure references, not verified top-template recommendations or purchased assets. No need to adopt Bootstrap/WordPress solely to reproduce familiar industry structure. Existing dependency-free renderer meets acceptance; revisit CMS/framework based on actual editing, listings or authenticated delivery needs.

## Persistent Crew widget

Floating launcher available throughout the page, client-appropriate colors and specialist identity. Contextual CTAs can open the same panel. Accessible name/state, keyboard access, Escape close, focus return, mobile usable layout, no content obstruction or unsolicited audio. Closing/reopening preserves the current conversation in page memory; reload is not persistent identity. Clearly disclose demo/digital-specialist status and action limits.

## Text to voice: approved product target

Two channels: **Talk here** (browser microphone) and **Call me** (explicit requested outbound callback). Both should continue the same business-specific specialist conversation. Carry a bounded summary of user intent, reviewed relevant facts and current request, with the client/workspace identity, role permissions and escalation rules. Confirm relevant context naturally before acting. Do not turn public source text or visitor messages into privileged instructions.

This milestone implements the transition experience/context seam and honest unavailable states only. No mic permission, phone capture, provider call or paid session. A unavailable option must explain status; never imply a call was placed or a voice integration is working.

Before live enablement: verified server-side provider integration; callback-specific explicit consent; validated destination handling; per-client permissions; identity/context isolation; bounded context/retention; finite attempt/rate/spend limits; no silent retry; user cancellation and connection/failure states; record actual provider outcomes; synthetic integration checks followed by separately authorized audible acceptance. Existing Retell choice and Autumn configuration remain preserved. Browser and telephone transitions require their own scoped provider validation; a prior voice allowance is not reusable.

## Acceptance and next scope

Three visibly distinct original niche structures; floating chat works after scrolling and from contextual CTAs; close/reopen keeps text context; keyboard/mobile behavior verified; voice options preserve context and honestly remain unavailable. Existing source-review, stale-artifact, role and local-draft gates retained. No public site publication, real prospects, outreach or remote access implementation.
