Approved baseline: owner described the modern residential realtor portfolio as much more premium on 2026-10-09. Preserve that visual direction and refine it selectively. The prior architectural-journal rejection remains historical evidence. Better tailoring, authentic content, logo treatment, image composition and interaction detail raise quality; adding decoration indefinitely does not.

## Small families, selected by brand fit

Start with three directions per supported niche. These are alternate layouts and content priorities, not color swaps. Select by the business's identity, customers, service mix and available verified assets; do not rotate randomly. Shared review, data, branding and Crew shell remain consistent. Current supported niches are real estate, cleaning and professional services. Avoid building a large catalog before representative concepts have been reviewed.

## Tailoring contract

Each reviewed business gets its own approved logo, colors, name, photography, supported service/fact copy, CTA objectives and specialist identity/role. Retain exact supplied logo proportions and artwork; use appropriate clear space and contrast. A mark can accompany the business name; a complete wordmark can stand alone with an accessible name. Keep a name fallback. No distorted, auto-recolored or stretched customer logos. The Cedar Lane demonstration mark is original fictional concept branding, not an authentic client asset.

Owner-uploaded logos are local raster assets with bounded size/dimensions. No third-party URL fetch and no executable SVG upload. Changing branding invalidates the concept review/hash just like other prospect inputs. After renderer changes, restart the local workbench, regenerate reviewed concepts and verify the actual preview tab; a stale imported module can otherwise show old HTML with new CSS.
