# Crew brand language

- Product/platform: **Crew**. Endorsement: **Crew by AW Creatives**.
- Legal company: **A. Wilcher Creatives LLC**. AWC is mostly internal shorthand.
- Category: **Digital Workforce**. Individual unit: **Specialist** or **Digital Team Member**. **AI Agent** is the technical term when it helps explain the technology.
- Customer Experience, Sales, Scheduling, Support and similar labels describe roles, not the product category. Avoid overusing receptionist publicly.
- Clients get their own customized specialists: identity, voice, personality, business knowledge, policies and permitted actions. They are not buying a copy of our demo persona.

## Demo identity

**Autumn Winters** is Crew's demo **Customer Experience Specialist**. Use Autumn conversationally. She is warm, capable, conversational and lightly witty; memorable without becoming gimmicky.

Optional only when direct-name conversation supports it: "Autumn Winters, actually. Yeah, I know — my creators had a sense of humor." Do not force or repeat the joke. Identify Autumn as an AI demo specialist where appropriate; never imply she is a human employee.

## Example copy

"Meet Crew by AW Creatives: a Digital Workforce shaped around your business."

"Talk to Autumn, our AI demo Customer Experience Specialist. Your specialist will be customized for your brand, services and workflows."

## Language to avoid

Avoid AWC as the public product name; Ava in current demo copy; receptionist as the whole category; selling Autumn as a universal client identity; human impersonation; and claims of live bookings, contacted humans or integrations when only demo proposals/local records exist. Keep fictional-business, AI and action disclosures concise.

## Configuration

Use generic configuration keys rather than hardcoded persona branches. See `crew/CLIENT-SPECIALIST-TEMPLATE.json`. This is a reusable planning template, not a live onboarding integration or automatically executable policy. Existing business facts and action approvals remain authoritative.
