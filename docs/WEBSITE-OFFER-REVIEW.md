# AWC offers and estimate review

Status: internal review draft, 2026-10-02. Monetary values are proposed USD planning amounts, unpublished and not approved sales terms. Existing live site remains unchanged until review. Phone, voice spending and generative AI activation are deferred.

## Customer experience

Explain the service, who it fits, tangible deliverables, scope assumptions and what changes the cost. Use “Explore your project budget” for the estimator. Give an itemized preliminary range; a written proposal confirms scope, price and timing after discovery. No contract, payment or booking occurs through an estimate. Carry the selections and server-calculated summary into an inquiry only with visitor consent.

## Proposed offers

| Offer | Draft budget | Included scope | Planning timeline |

These are AWC planning proposals, not claims about typical market prices. Broader branding, e-commerce, custom applications, voice agents and complex integrations need individual discovery. A prospect can request any service without purchasing a planning session first.

## Estimator rules

Website offers only: zero to five additional pages at $150–$250 each; zero to five pages of copy assistance at $100–$150 each. Base scope assumes supplied brand assets/content, one language, no store, payment processing, account portal or bespoke integration. Add-ons must fit the stated scope; substantial complexity goes to discovery. Server validates bounded integer selections and recomputes the total before saving the inquiry. It never accepts a client-entered total as authoritative. No estimate for arbitrary custom work.

Example: Launch Website + two additional pages + three pages of copy assistance = $2,100–$3,450. That range excludes domain, hosting, licensed assets, subscriptions, provider usage and applicable taxes/fees; these must be identified in the written proposal. Do not invent discounts, refund terms, deposits, delivery availability or support response guarantees.

## Delivery and commercial review

Before publishing amounts, owner confirms delivery capacity, scope, prices and internal economics. Record actual setup and support effort on first projects. A fixed planning-session price requires the owner to approve the session and written deliverable. Care has no unlimited changes, 24/7 support, uptime SLA, paid campaigns or included third-party fees. No automatic renewal or cancellation policy is asserted before terms are approved.

Website inquiry handling here means prepared answers and deterministic estimates, not generative AI or telephone reception. Voice pilot scope remains governed by existing pilot readiness documents. All client deployments require feasibility, access, acceptance and delivery validation.

## Inquiry handling acceptance

Visitors can understand services, fit, scope, budget assumptions, process, content needs, revisions, maintenance and human routing. Unknown matters route clearly to the studio. Prepared answers do not claim contracts, ownership transfer, refunds, response deadlines or appointments that have not been agreed. Measure the 80–90% self-service goal later using anonymized categories and confirmed outcomes; do not publish it as achieved.
