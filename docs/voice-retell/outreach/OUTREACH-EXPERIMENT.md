# First outreach experiment — design only

## Channel and budget recommendation

Default to manual personalized business email where the address is published for appropriate business contact and channel use is cleared. The rationale is reviewable personalization, low cash cost and asynchronous response; this is an AWC hypothesis, not a researched claim of superior conversion.

Discover candidates manually from business websites and local directories/groups; no scraping, membership solicitation or lead-list purchases. Use an active business FB/IG account as an alternative initial channel only when email is unsuitable and rules allow it. Contact forms are a lower-priority alternative only if vendor inquiries are appropriate. LinkedIn is useful only where an owner actually uses it. Direct phone/voicemail is optional later after channel clearance and explicit calling authorization; never automated.

Recommended sequence: verify business → choose one suitable channel → ask permission to show brief proof → relevant walkthrough → response-driven fit call → conditional feasibility. Do not send email, DM, form and voicemail in parallel to one owner. Follow-ups stay on the chosen channel unless the prospect requests a change.

Cash target $0 using existing tools; proposed upper planning budget $50, **not spend authority**. No ads, list tools, new number, subscriptions or paid calls. If paid voice proof is later wanted, authorize a separate numeric budget and allowance; do not silently charge it to this test. Reserve the $50 rather than inventing a purchase. Track preparation/operator time even when cash spend is zero.

## Before release — all OPEN until recorded

Owner accepts narrow offer, capped pricing structure and support capacity; one geographic market selected; current channel/jurisdiction and platform requirements reviewed; identity/footer/contact/opt-out details completed; recipients and personalized text approved; explicit contact authorization recorded. Approved offline proof prepared. A yes to this document is not authorization to send.

## Tiny staged test

1. In the next milestone, manually screen 10 public-qualified businesses using the scorecard. No need to prepare a large reserve list.
2. After explicit send authorization, assign 5 to angle A (coverage while busy), 5 to angle B (routine-question interruptions). Keep channel, offer, CTA and other copy stable. Where both angles fit, alternate assignment; where only one fits, record selection bias. Never force an irrelevant message for balance.
3. Touches: day 0 initial, day 3 useful follow-up, day 8 final close; day 12 review. Business days/local hours as appropriate. Stop immediately for opt-out, refusal, bounce or reply; reply initiates a human conversation rather than the scheduled sequence.
4. First checkpoint after 10 initial messages and their response window. Expand only through explicit authorization, to at most 20 unique prospects total. No automatic next batch.

## Measurement ledger

Track counts and dates: attempted, delivered (attempted minus known bounces; inbox placement unknown), replies, positive relevant replies, demo invitations accepted, demos completed, owner-qualified conversations, conditional pilot interest, declines, opt-outs/complaints, operator minutes and approved spend. Positive reply means acknowledges relevant need or accepts a relevant next step; an auto-reply is not positive. Count unique businesses, not repeated touches. Record objection reasons verbatim only in restricted notes, then summarize for Git.

Rates: positive replies / delivered; completed demos / accepted invitations; qualified conversations / completed demos; conditional interest / qualified conversations. If denominator zero, report N/A. Never equate reply with client or conditional interest with revenue. Avoid open tracking as a success metric; do not require tracking pixels.

## Proposed directional thresholds, not industry benchmarks

- Any complaint or opt-out mishandling: pause the sequence, correct contact policy/suppression before resuming. A single opt-out is honored immediately; repeated relevance objections trigger fit review.
- Two hard bounces in the first 10: stop and verify contact sourcing; do not rotate identities to bypass rejection.
- Zero positive relevant replies after all 10 complete the window: stop expansion, review fit/observation/CTA and channel suitability. Change one factor; do not buy ads to compensate.
- At least two positive relevant replies and one completed demo, with no unresolved channel problem: enough to propose up to 10 more, not proof of conversion effectiveness.
- Replies but no completed demos: inspect friction and demo-mode expectations. Demos but no qualified interest: inspect fit, proof gap and delivery concern rather than intensifying pressure.
- At 20 maximum: close the experiment and report denominators, costs/time and reasons. Small samples cannot establish a winning variant or expected acquisition cost.

Scaling requires repeated owner-confirmed pain, qualified demo progression, delivery feasibility, support capacity and acceptable measured acquisition effort. Paid acquisition requires a separate decision after at least one completed pilot supplies outcome and cost evidence, truthful proof, a working conversion path and an approved cash/loss limit. Neither 20 replies nor a signed conditional pilot alone justifies ads.

Progress / Cost Gate: stop after two focused unsuccessful attempts, two substantial runs without validated progress, usage-cap hits or deteriorating evidence/cost; record the blocker and choose a cheaper decisive test. This milestone stops at preparation; no actual prospects, sends or provider usage were created.
