# Demo hook: let the owner test the relevant boundary

“What happens to estimate calls while your team is cleaning? If that is a problem, let me show you a three-minute example: a caller gets a service explanation, a bounded estimate and an appointment request—with the points that still require a person made visible.”

Then disclose: “Ava represents fictional BrightHome. These are sample facts, prices and slots. This offline walkthrough makes no real booking or human contact and does not play audio.”

## Two-to-five-minute guided proof

| Time | Owner-relevant scene | Honest proof boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Ask which repeated question or coverage gap matters; introduce Ava | Do not diagnose the business from its website |
| 0:30–1:15 | Standard versus deep cleaning; ask about oven cleaning or unsupported service | Approved catalog; unknowns/add-on pricing defer to a person |
| 1:15–1:45 | Three-bedroom deep estimate ($225 fictional fixture) | Only standard/deep rules; prices and insurance do not transfer to a client |
| 1:45–2:45 | Sample availability → proposal → local approval → duplicate/stale rejection | Mock calendar and scripted approval, not real calendar integration or voice-page approval |
| 2:45–3:30 | Reveal retained request, estimate and human-follow-up context | Local record, no delivered message or live transfer; synthetic excerpt is not a recorded call |
| 3:30–4:30 | Discuss interruption and the owner's desired outcome | Historical barge-in acceptance is separate evidence; offline output proves no audible behavior |

Use [existing script](../DEMO-SCRIPT.md) and [runbook](../OPERATOR-RUNBOOK.md). Target duration is proposed, not measured. For a two-minute version, show the owner's top pain, one matching scene and one safety boundary; do not claim all scenes fit. Invite the owner to select among the prepared examples rather than implying arbitrary live questions work in the scripted terminal output.

Close: “Which part would change your current workflow, and what would need to be different for your business?” If a fit exists: “Would you like to check the requirements for a paid 14-day pilot?” If no need exists, thank them and stop.
