# First-Pilot Prospect Qualification & Outreach Preparation

2026-09-28 • Preparation only • No outreach or spend authorized

## Decision in one minute

Start with one local residential cleaning company whose owner confirms that routine inbound calls interrupt work or wait unanswered. Offer a short, clearly fictional BrightHome walkthrough, then a fit discussion for a conditional paid 14-day pilot. Sell an owner-defined improvement in call handling, not AI novelty or guaranteed revenue.

Use a manual email-first experiment with 10 prospects, expandable to 20 only through the checkpoint below. One observed fact and one relevant question per message. Do not assume a listed phone number means missed calls. No public price, self-service demo phone number, production-readiness claim or guaranteed launch date.
