# First-pilot prospect profile

## One narrow target

An owner-operated local residential cleaning business in one selected service market and time zone, serving English-speaking inbound callers. A public service menu and business phone invite estimate/appointment inquiries. Prefer routine standard/deep/recurring cleaning, a bounded service area, and a reachable owner or small office team. Geography is not yet selected; do not assume the user's location or fictional Springfield is the target.

Public eligibility is provisional. A small team does not prove limited phone coverage. A contact form does not prove poor follow-up. Reviews are not proof of missed calls. Record observations separately from hypotheses; confirm workflow in discovery.

## Required for conditional pilot fit

- Recurring inbound demand and an owner-confirmed coverage/interruption problem worth measuring; aim for at least 20 eligible calls in 14 days, without guaranteeing volume.
- At most six clearly approved services, two add-ons and two deterministic estimate rules. If all prices require inspection, do not sell automated quotes; first-pilot priority goes to simpler businesses.
- One authoritative calendar, straightforward durations/buffers and no crew/travel optimization. Confirm human approval workflow feasibility later; request-only scope requires explicit re-signing.
- Owner can approve facts, review calls daily, attend day 3/7/14 reviews and name primary/backup humans with realistic callback targets.
- Willingness to consider a fixed paid 14-day pilot with setup component, capped minutes, explicit limits and no automatic renewal; final price follows feasibility.
- One overflow OR after-hours forwarding path. Existing number remains client-controlled. Readiness and privacy gates precede live operation.

## Exclude / hold

Exclude enterprise procurement, multiple-location dispatch, emergency work, sensitive account changes, payments, unsupported languages, complex crew scheduling, hazardous work and demands for autonomous booking or guaranteed revenue. Exclude no reliable human fallback. Hold unknown facts for discovery rather than marking them true. Low call volume may fit a later learning exercise, but is a poor first evidence-producing pilot. A functioning receptionist with no meaningful unmet need is not a pain to manufacture.

## Personalization rule

Record one source URL, date and exact factual observation: e.g., a service page lists recurring cleaning and invites phone estimates. Turn the possible gap into a question: “Who handles estimate calls while the team is cleaning?” Do not pretend to have called, audited response times or seen internal lost leads. Select one pain angle per prospect. Use [scorecard](QUALIFICATION-SCORECARD.md) before allocating demo time.
