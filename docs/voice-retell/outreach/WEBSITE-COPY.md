# Minimal website/demo copy recommendations

Recommendations only; no website files inspected or modified because this milestone restricts source reads and excludes redesign. Apply only where the existing page lacks these messages; this is not a claim that its current copy is defective.

## Narrow copy set

**Headline:** Keep routine cleaning inquiries moving while your team is on the job.

**Supporting line:** Explore an AI receptionist pilot for approved service answers, bounded estimates and clearer appointment requests—with people handling exceptions.

**Primary CTA:** See the cleaning-business walkthrough.

**Adjacent disclosure:** Fictional BrightHome demo with sample prices and appointment data. The current walkthrough is offline and makes no real bookings or human contact.

**Three proof labels:** Service answers from approved facts · Estimates within defined rules · Appointment safeguards and callback context.

**Pilot section:** We're preparing one local residential-cleaning pilot: one inbound coverage path for 14 days, with reviews on days 3, 7 and 14. Scope, setup, capped usage and price are agreed after fit and feasibility checks. Client integrations and launch approval are required.

**Secondary CTA:** Discuss pilot fit.

## Implementation boundary

Use an existing valid inquiry route for a later approved implementation; do not add a nonfunctional button, invented demo number or new data-collection form here. A live voice CTA stays absent until access, budget and readiness are separately approved. Avoid client logos/testimonials without permission, public price anchors, guaranteed bookings/savings, “never miss a call,” “replace staff,” “instant setup” or 24/7 reliability claims. No layout, branding, tracking or broad content work required.
