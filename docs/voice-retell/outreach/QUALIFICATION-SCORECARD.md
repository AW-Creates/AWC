# First-pilot qualification scorecard

Blank operational template; no real prospects collected. A score prioritizes review, not permission to contact or launch. Use [CSV](PROSPECT-SCORECARD.csv) in restricted working storage for real records.

## Evidence discipline

For each criterion record status **observed / owner-confirmed / unknown / disqualifying**, source URL or conversation date, short evidence and reviewer. Unknown = U, not zero and not a positive assumption. Do not infer staffing, missed calls, revenue, budget or call volume from public appearance. Do not collect personal vulnerabilities or unnecessary personal data.

## Stage 1: public screen, maximum 10

| Criterion | 2 points | 1 point | 0 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential cleaning fit | Explicit core residential standard/deep/recurring services | Residential alongside other services, focus uncertain | Clearly another category |
| Local scope | One explicitly identified service market | Local footprint but geographic simplicity unclear | Explicit complex multi-location operation |
| Phone inquiry path | Public business phone explicitly invites estimates/bookings | Public business phone without stated inquiry purpose | No permitted business inquiry channel identified |
| Service clarity | Clear service scope and exclusions/FAQ | Service names only | No usable service information found after bounded review |
| Appointment fit | Explicit routine scheduled residential visits | Appointments mentioned but workflow unclear | Explicit emergency/complex dispatch requirement |

Use U when not enough evidence to choose. Prioritize 8–10/10 with all five fields evidenced and no exclusion. Scores 6–7 or any U: manual clarification queue, not “highly qualified.” Below 6: defer for this first batch. Explicit hard exclusions override any score. These are internal triage thresholds, not market-tested predictors. Public-qualified means suitable for discovery, not pilot-qualified.

## Stage 2: owner-confirmed fit, maximum 10

Award 2 = clear fit, 1 = partial fit with a concrete resolution, 0 = incompatible, U = unknown.

| Criterion | Clear-fit evidence |
|---|---|
| Need and volume | Owner names meaningful pain and plausibly at least 20 eligible calls over 14 days |
| Facts and estimates | Approved narrow catalog and up to two bounded rules; exceptions identified |
| Scheduling simplicity | One calendar, simple durations/buffers, human approval requirement accepted |
| Human coverage | Named primary/backup, workable callback expectations and daily review capacity |
| Pilot commitment | Willing to consider paid capped 14-day scope and day 3/7/14 reviews; outcome defined |

8–10 with no U/0 and all mandatory offer criteria met = conditional feasibility candidate. A 1 requires a written resolution before agreement. 6–7 or U = HOLD for specific answers; below 6 or incompatible hard criterion = decline/defer. Reliable fallback, explicit scope acceptance, client-approved facts and production gates cannot be waived by aggregate score. Price acceptance follows an actual quote; willingness to discuss price is not a purchase.

## Required row fields

Business, public website, selected market, public business channel, observation/source/date, hypothesis/question, five public scores, evidence statuses, public disposition, five discovery scores, owner-confirmed need/volume, exclusions, unknowns, one message angle, variant, demo mode, outreach permission, contact history, opt-out, next action. Add criterion-specific source/date notes in evidence_notes; no fabricated sample rows.
