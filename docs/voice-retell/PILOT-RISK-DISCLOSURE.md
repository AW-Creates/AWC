# Pilot risk and disclosure launch checklist

Requirement checklist, not legal advice or a determination of applicable law. Client jurisdiction and caller locations are unknown. Qualified jurisdiction-specific review is required before production recording/telephony decisions; this milestone conducts no legal research or drafting.

- [ ] AI identity: approved greeting clearly identifies an AI receptionist; no human impersonation or claim of independent professional expertise.
- [ ] Consent: reviewer determines applicable recording, transcription, notice and consent requirements across relevant jurisdictions, including refusal handling. Do not assume recording-off eliminates all privacy obligations. No launch while unresolved.
- [ ] PII: collect minimum name/contact/service details only when necessary and approved; restrict access, redact logs, approve vendors/storage/retention/deletion and test controls. Avoid unnecessary sensitive details.
- [ ] Facts: owner signs versioned business facts, prices and policy evidence; unresolved contradictions go to human review. Demo insurance and fictional dates never become client statements.
- [ ] Prohibited claims: no invented price, availability, discount, insurance, guarantee, professional advice, promised savings or emergency response.
- [ ] Urgent/emergency calls: not an emergency service; use approved direction to appropriate local emergency services for immediate danger, without diagnosis, triage or dispatch promises; notify human via approved route when appropriate.
- [ ] Payments/billing: explain approved payment methods only; no card/bank collection, payment authorization, refunds or balance changes. Disputes go to a human.
- [ ] Identity-sensitive actions: no account access, disclosure of another customer's details or sensitive changes based on caller ID or claimed identity; require approved human process.
- [ ] Human escalation: clear caller request path, primary/backup staffing, receipt verification and timeout fallback; no claim of successful transfer when only a local record exists.
- [ ] Reliability/security: stable signed callbacks, restricted authenticated booking approval, outage routing, secure credentials and tested rollback; no unsupported uptime commitment.
- [ ] Spend and scope: approved test/pilot caps, cutoff reserve and no automatic overage/renewal; preserve the consumed demo allowance.

Evidence fields per item: responsible owner, approval reference, config version, verification date and PASS/HOLD. Critical privacy/security/booking requirements cannot be waived; remove the affected feature through explicit re-scope or delay launch.
