# Crew recording operator runbook

1. Recover PROJECT_STATE.md, CLOSEOUT.md and RECORDING-PROGRESS.json. All ten existing paid attempts are consumed. Never reset ledgers, overwrite approval pins or automatically retry failures.
2. For a future revision, pin role identity, approved knowledge, tools/permissions, published provider version, voice and three questions. Obtain actual voice/question listening acceptance and a new finite allowance before recording. Keep raw samples/transcripts/provider IDs and credentials ignored locally.
3. Run the free preflight and relevant gate tests. Readiness must bind exact accepted source hashes, expiry, numerical rate and session/cost caps. Unused readiness renewal is append-only and cannot revive consumed grants. Original record-approved-role.cjs, record-approved-retake.cjs and record-approved-autumn-corrected.cjs profiles must reject reuse of existing consumed grants.
4. Supervise genuine remote speech capture with the same accepted presenter audio sent to the agent. Audio activity is heuristic, not proof of semantic turn completion. End on overlap, error, silence or cap; reconcile provider terminal cost before another separately approved attempt. Preserve failed evidence privately.
5. Prepare a private review with prepare-review.cjs, transcribe-local.cjs and review-server.cjs as needed. Check full question/reply completeness, identity, limits, audio, caption wording and timing. Owner listening acceptance is mandatory; never manufacture it from test success or ASR.
6. Finalize only accepted takes with finalize-media.cjs. Verify exact paired MP4/VTT hashes, complete decode, cue order/bounds and size/duration constraints. Use upload-hosted.cjs's explicit reviewed manifest; do not copy raw captures into public directories.
7. Publish the validated Sites source and bounded media upload window. Remove temporary upload credentials immediately afterwards. Explicitly select reviewed role revisions; check all hosted hashes, ranges and304 plus upload403. Keep public live-call gates disabled unless separately authorized and validated.
8. Test deployed desktop/mobile playback, captions, seeking/replay/end, close/pause/source cleanup, restored focus and CTA context. Block provider/microphone/business mutations; separately classify blocked same-origin platform traffic. Inspect screenshots and the actual public page. Update closeout/state, commit, push safely and verify local/origin/fresh remote equality and clean tree.
