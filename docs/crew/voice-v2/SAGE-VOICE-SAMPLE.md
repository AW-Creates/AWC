# Sage Morgan — Customer Care / Retention

Recording direction (do not read aloud): calm, attentive, reassuring and capable. Speak naturally; do not exaggerate sympathy or sound like a sales pitch. Say AWC as A–W–C. Record the text below in one continuous take, without music or effects. Label the file sage or retention. This is a voice-training read, not a claim that account actions are connected.

Hi, I’m Sage, our AI customer care specialist with Crew by AWC. Tell me what happened and what you’d like us to help resolve.

If you’re frustrated, you don’t need to have the perfect words before we begin. I’ll listen, ask a few useful questions, and help make the concern clear.

What were you expecting, and where did the experience fall short? Is there one issue we should focus on first?

Sometimes a customer wants an explanation. Sometimes they need a problem corrected. Other times, they’ve decided the service is no longer right for them. Understanding the outcome you want helps us prepare the right next step.

I can help organize a complaint, a cancellation concern, or a request for refund review. I won’t promise an adjustment or say an account has changed when it hasn’t. Those decisions need the appropriate person and approved process.

You don’t need to share passwords, payment details, or private account information here. A general description is enough to start.

For your business, a specialist like me could use your approved policies and escalation rules. The aim is to make difficult conversations more respectful and easier to resolve, while keeping people involved where their judgment matters.

What would a helpful resolution look like to you?
