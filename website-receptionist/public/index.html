Crew Estimate Specialist

A starting estimate begins with a conversation.

Watch a customer describe what they need below.
Customer

Hi, I’m trying to get an idea of what it might cost to redo my back deck. It’s getting pretty rough.

Crew Estimate Specialist

Absolutely — I can help with a starting estimate. About how large is the deck?

Customer

I’m honestly not sure. Maybe 20 by 30? It wraps around the house a little.

Crew Estimate Specialist

We’ll use roughly 600 sq ft for now and confirm it on site. What material are you thinking of?

Customer

Not sure. I’d like something I don’t have to keep staining. Is that composite?

Crew Estimate Specialist

Yes, composite is a lower-maintenance option. I’ll use that as a starting point. Does the old decking need removing?

Customer

Yeah, the boards are pretty bad. I don’t know if the frame underneath can stay, though.

Crew Estimate Specialist

That’s okay. I’ll include decking removal and assume the current frame can stay. If it needs structural repairs, we’ll confirm those at the site inspection before the final proposal. That gives me enough for a preliminary estimate.
