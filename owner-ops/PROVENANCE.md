# Prospect Demo Factory provenance rules

Every approved factual assertion must reference a source ID and appear in that source's manually captured excerpt. The operator must review the source and confirm the configuration. A matching excerpt is a traceability check, not independent verification of truth or ownership. Do not treat an arbitrary pasted excerpt as verified evidence.

For real businesses use publicly accessible, credential-free source URLs and capture dates. Confirm identity, services and FAQ answers directly from the source. Use original factual summaries as copy; do not reproduce the source site's prose or layout. Source excerpts stay in the operator configuration and are not published as website copy. Do not scrape at scale.

Mark uncertain claims `uncertain` and unsupported claims `unsupported`. The generator omits them from rendered content and Crew knowledge and lists them in the operator summary. A human must check the public evidence before changing their status to `approved`. Missing hours, team members, credentials, testimonials, sales results, inventory or listings remain absent. Do not manufacture a claim because the design has a space for it.

The three proof businesses are fictional: their `.example` records are declarations for product QA, not fetched public evidence or real prospects. Cedar Lane remains the existing fictional fixture. Fictional declarations cannot be used as public evidence for a real business.

Assets must have a local approved path, `concept` or `generated` kind, and a visible concept/generated label. The existing residence imagery is illustrative concept imagery, not a listing, proof of ownership or picture of an actual client/team. Operator review is required before using uploaded brand assets or personal likenesses. No current listing, valuation, agent credential, testimonial or performance claim is generated.

Configurations contain no secrets or provider bindings. Paid channels, real booking and payments remain disabled in this bounded factory. A later explicit enablement milestone must review live actions and dedicated provider configuration separately. Loopback-only access supplies privacy here; noindex alone does not protect a remotely deployed demo.
